Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026  ·  Zeltix Craft

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeltix Craft ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you use our website and download our digital products.

Short version: we collect your email address to send you the free guide you requested and, with your consent, occasional updates. We do not sell your data. You can unsubscribe at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to deliver our service:

  • Email address — when you sign up to receive our free guide or newsletter.
  • First name (optional) — to personalise emails if you choose to provide it.
  • Purchase information — processed by Gumroad. We do not store payment details.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics (page views, referral source) via privacy-friendly tools. No cross-site tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the free guide you requested.
  • To send the email sequence you opted into (educational content and product updates).
  • To improve our content and understand what topics are most useful.
  • To fulfill purchases made through Gumroad.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

3. Email Communications

By signing up, you consent to receive the email sequence described at the point of sign-up. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe at any time by emailing hello@zeltixcraft.com.

Our email platform is Beehiiv. Your email address is stored on Beehiiv's servers in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Beehiiv — email delivery and newsletter management (beehiiv.com/privacy)
  • Gumroad — payment processing and product delivery (gumroad.com/privacy)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (GitHub Privacy Statement)

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties.

5. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. Basic session functionality may use a browser's local storage — no personal data is stored in this way.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you are subscribed to our newsletter. Upon unsubscribing, your data is removed from our active mailing list within 30 days. You may request complete deletion at any time (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@zeltixcraft.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to active subscribers. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent version.
